# Growth Strategy Planning

Use this skill when a growth question needs a classic method, a structured planning artifact or a decision SOP rather than another raw metric. Read `references/classic-growth-methods.md` and select the smallest method combination that can change the current decision.

This skill produces worksheets, evidence matrices, hypotheses, experiments, priorities and review materials. It does not interview users, browse websites, contact prospects, change product code, change prices or execute campaigns.

## Method routing

| User problem | Preferred method pack | First output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product value is unclear | Value Proposition Canvas + Lean Canvas | Value / business assumption map |
| Registration grows but users do not reach value | Aha Moment / Activation Event | Activation definition and event plan |
| Churn is rising or unexplained | Churn Taxonomy / Win-back | Churn segments and recovery hypotheses |
| Too many possible channels | Bullseye Framework | Candidate / test / focus channel map |
| Teams jump from problem to features | Opportunity Solution Tree | Outcome → opportunity → solution → experiment tree |
| Interviews are full of opinions | The Mom Test / Switch Interview | Evidence-led interview guide |
| Users share but growth quality is unknown | Referral Loop / K-factor | Referral loop and quality metrics |
| Market opportunity is uncertain | TAM / SAM / SOM | Assumption-backed market range |
| Pricing or packaging is unclear | Van Westendorp / Gabor-Granger | Pricing research plan |
| B2B pipeline is opaque | B2B Revenue Funnel / MEDDICC | Stage definitions and evidence gaps |
| Users or revenue change without explanation | Growth Accounting | User / revenue growth bridge |
| Decisions disappear after reviews | OKR / A3 / OODA / Decision Log | Operating decision record |

## SOP gates

### Gate 0 — Define the decision

State the business outcome, target segment, current growth stage, decision date and what will change if the answer is known. Reject method shopping without a decision.

### Gate 1 — Choose the minimum method set

Choose one primary method and at most two supporting methods. Use `not-applicable` when the business model does not fit. Do not combine Lean Canvas, OKR, A3 and a full business plan unless a specific decision requires each one.

### Gate 2 — Separate evidence from assumptions

For every input, mark `observed`, `user-reported`, `estimated`, `inferred` or `missing`. Link evidence to a local note, dataset, interview record or approved source. Missing evidence becomes a question or validation task, not a fabricated value.

### Gate 3 — Produce the planning artifact

Return the selected method, filled fields, unresolved fields, trade-offs and the decision rule. Use the corresponding section in `references/classic-growth-methods.md`. Keep the artifact short enough to review in one sitting.

### Gate 4 — Connect to metrics and experiments

Link the plan to one primary outcome, one or two guardrails, an observation window and a data source. Convert the highest-risk assumption into HADI; use RICE / ICE only after reach, impact, confidence and effort are explicit.

### Gate 5 — Review and decide

End with one of: `continue`, `test`, `revise`, `defer`, `stop` or `needs-evidence`. Record owner, date, evidence, decision and next review. A completed canvas or interview guide is not business impact.

## Output contract

Return in this order:

1. decision and selected method;
2. evidence / assumption matrix;
3. planning artifact;
4. risks, exclusions and missing inputs;
5. metric, guardrail and validation plan;
6. prioritized next actions and decision date.

When the result affects Acquisition, Activation, Retention, Referral or Revenue, connect it back to `growth_review`, `growth_experiment`, `growth_prioritize`, `growth_cohort_analyze` or `growth_economics` as appropriate.

Never present a framework completion score as product-market fit, causality, market revenue, pricing truth or growth impact.
